Tilt Checkpoint Endless+

Tilt your phone to roll the ball into each glowing checkpoint before time runs out. Survive endless handcrafted stages, collect 3 checkpoints per stage, then enter the finish portal to advance. A red chaser ball hunts you each stage.

Android/mobile: tap Enable Tilt, then Start Game.
Desktop fallback: use arrow keys or WASD.
Each stage has 3 fixed checkpoints. After the third one, a finish portal appears.
The red chaser speeds up every stage. If it catches you, you lose time and it disappears until the next stage.
Hazards still remove time and knock the ball back. Portal clears add bonus time.
